DSCR Loans for Short-Term Rentals: Airbnb Financing in LA

By Harout — Sunny Home Loans·6 min read

Short-term rentals can generate significantly more monthly income than a traditional long-term lease in the right Los Angeles neighborhoods — but that income used to be nearly impossible to use for loan qualification. DSCR programs have changed that, with several lenders now accepting projected short-term rental income directly.

Why Traditional Financing Struggles With STR Income

Conventional lenders are built around signed, 12-month leases with predictable monthly rent. Short-term rental income doesn't fit that model — it fluctuates by season, by night, and by platform, which makes it difficult to document in a way conventional underwriting recognizes. Many long-term-rental-focused DSCR lenders have historically had the same limitation.

How STR-Friendly DSCR Lenders Calculate Income

Rather than requiring a signed lease, STR-friendly DSCR programs typically use data from a service like AirDNA to project the property's realistic market rental income based on comparable short-term rental listings nearby.

  • For a purchase: Projected income from comparable listings is generally used, since there's no existing rental history on the property yet
  • For a refinance on a property you're already operating as an STR: Actual booking history can sometimes be used in addition to or instead of the projection, particularly if it demonstrates income above the market projection
  • DSCR calculation: The same core formula applies — projected monthly income divided by the monthly PITIA payment

Where This Works Best in the LA Market

Properties near entertainment districts, tourist corridors, and event venues tend to project the highest short-term rental income — which makes areas like Burbank near the studios, and parts of the San Fernando Valley within reach of major attractions, worth a closer look for investors specifically pursuing an STR strategy.

What to Confirm Before You Buy

  • Local short-term rental regulations. Rules vary significantly by city — some require registration, permits, or even primary residency, which can eliminate a pure-investment STR strategy in certain areas.
  • HOA restrictions, if the property is a condo or part of a planned community — many explicitly prohibit short-term rentals regardless of what city law allows.
  • Realistic occupancy assumptions. AirDNA projections are estimates based on comparable listings, not guarantees — factoring in a reasonable vacancy buffer when running your own numbers is worth doing even after the lender's calculation.

Is an STR DSCR Loan Right for Your Strategy?

If you've identified a property where realistic short-term rental income would meaningfully outperform a long-term lease — and you've confirmed local regulations allow it — an STR-friendly DSCR loan can unlock financing that wasn't available even a few years ago. It's a more specialized product than a standard long-term-rental DSCR loan, so working with a lender who explicitly supports STR income calculation matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do lenders calculate income for a short-term rental DSCR loan?
Most lenders use projected income data from a service like AirDNA, which estimates market rental income for short-term rentals based on comparable listings in the area, rather than requiring a signed long-term lease.
Do I need an existing Airbnb track record to qualify?
Not necessarily. For a purchase, projected income from a service like AirDNA is often sufficient. For a refinance on a property you already operate as a short-term rental, actual booking history can sometimes be used instead.
Are short-term rentals legal everywhere in Los Angeles?
No — short-term rental rules vary significantly by city within the LA area, including registration requirements and, in some cases, primary residency requirements. Local regulations should be confirmed before purchasing with a short-term rental strategy in mind.
