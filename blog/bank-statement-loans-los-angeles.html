Bank Statement Loans for Self-Employed Borrowers in Los Angeles

By Harout — Sunny Home Loans·7 min read

If you're self-employed in Los Angeles, you already know the problem: your business does well, but your tax returns don't show it. Between legitimate write-offs, depreciation, and business expenses, your net income on paper can look nothing like what actually lands in your account each month. A bank statement loan is built specifically to close that gap.

Why Tax Returns Undersell Self-Employed Borrowers

Conventional lenders qualify you using your adjusted gross income — the number left after your CPA has done everything possible to legally minimize your tax bill. That's smart tax strategy, but it's terrible mortgage strategy. A restaurant owner grossing $400,000 a year might show $60,000 in net income after write-offs, which is nowhere near enough to qualify for the home their actual cash flow supports.

Bank statement loans sidestep this entirely by qualifying you on deposits instead of net income after deductions.

How the Qualifying Income Is Calculated

Lenders review either your personal or business bank statements — usually 12 or 24 months — and average your monthly deposits. For business accounts, an expense ratio is applied to estimate what portion of those deposits represents real income versus business overhead.

  • Sole proprietors: A standard 50% expense factor is common unless a CPA-prepared profit and loss statement supports a lower figure.
  • Personal accounts: If your income is deposited directly into a personal account, the full deposit amount is often usable without an expense deduction.
  • 24-month averaging: Longer statement history smooths out seasonal swings — useful for LA business owners in hospitality, real estate, or entertainment-adjacent industries where income fluctuates month to month.

What Los Angeles Lenders Typically Require in 2026

  1. Minimum 2 years self-employed in the same field, verified by a CPA letter or business license
  2. 620 minimum credit score, though 680+ gets meaningfully better pricing
  3. 12 or 24 months of statements from the same account(s) with no major unexplained gaps
  4. Down payment of 10–20% depending on loan amount and credit profile
  5. Reserves — typically 2–6 months of the housing payment in verified assets

Why This Matters More in LA Than Almost Anywhere Else

Los Angeles has one of the highest concentrations of self-employed professionals, freelancers, and small business owners in the country — real estate agents, entertainment industry contractors, consultants, and restaurant and retail owners all share the same qualifying problem. Combine that with LA's high property values, and the gap between what a self-employed buyer actually earns and what their tax returns show can mean the difference between qualifying for a starter condo and qualifying for the home that matches their real income.

Common Mistakes That Slow Down a Bank Statement File

  • Mixing personal and business deposits in the same account without clear documentation, which can confuse the income calculation
  • Large, unexplained deposits close to application — lenders will ask for a source, so it's worth timing big transfers before you start the process
  • Switching bank accounts mid-application, which restarts the statement history clock

Is a Bank Statement Loan Right for You?

If your bank balance tells a better story than your tax return does, a bank statement loan is likely your most direct path to qualifying for what you actually earn. It carries a modest rate premium over conventional financing, but for most self-employed LA borrowers, the ability to qualify at all — or qualify for meaningfully more — outweighs the difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do lenders look at personal or business bank statements?
Either can work. Business statements are common for owners of established companies, while personal statements are often used by freelancers, consultants, and 1099 contractors who deposit income directly.
How many months of bank statements do I need?
Most Los Angeles lenders ask for 12 or 24 months. Twenty-four months of history typically unlocks better pricing than the 12-month minimum.
Will a few slow deposit months disqualify me?
Usually not. Lenders average your deposits across the full statement period, so seasonal dips are absorbed into the average rather than reviewed month by month.
