DSCR Loan Requirements 2026: What LA Investors Need to Know

By Harout — Sunny Home Loans·8 min read

If you're a real estate investor in Los Angeles or the Santa Clarita Valley, you've probably run into the same wall every self-employed borrower does: traditional lenders want two years of tax returns, W-2s, and debt-to-income ratios that don't reflect how investment properties actually make money. A DSCR loan sidesteps all of that. Here's exactly what qualifies in 2026.

What Is a DSCR Loan?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Instead of qualifying you based on your personal income, a DSCR loan qualifies the property based on its rental income relative to its monthly debt obligations. If the property's rent covers the mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, and HOA dues, you can usually qualify — regardless of what your personal tax returns show.

This makes DSCR loans a go-to option for:

  • Self-employed investors whose tax returns show heavy write-offs
  • Investors scaling a rental portfolio who've maxed out conventional loan limits
  • Out-of-state or first-time investors buying in the LA market
  • Anyone buying through an LLC for liability protection

How DSCR Is Calculated

The formula is simple:

DSCR = Monthly Gross Rental Income ÷ Monthly PITIA

(PITIA = Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance, and Association dues, if applicable)

  • DSCR of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the housing payment.
  • DSCR above 1.0 means the property cash-flows positive.
  • DSCR below 1.0 doesn't automatically disqualify you — many lenders allow ratios down to 0.75, though pricing and down payment requirements adjust accordingly.

2026 DSCR Loan Requirements

While every lender's guidelines vary slightly, here's what's typical for qualifying in the current market:

  1. Credit score: 660 minimum, though 700+ gets meaningfully better pricing
  2. Down payment: 20–25% for a 1.0+ DSCR; some programs allow lower with strong compensating factors
  3. DSCR ratio: Most programs accept 0.75–1.25, with the best rates at 1.0+
  4. Reserves: Typically 3–6 months of PITIA in reserve, verified by bank statements
  5. Property type: Single-family, 2–4 unit, condos, and some short-term rental (STR) properties are eligible
  6. No personal income documentation: No tax returns, no W-2s, no employment verification
  7. Entity vesting: Loans can typically close in an LLC name, which most conventional programs don't allow

Why LA and Santa Clarita Investors Are Using DSCR Loans Right Now

Rental rates across the 818 and 661 corridors have stayed strong even as conventional lending has tightened, which means more properties are cash-flowing well enough to hit that 1.0+ DSCR threshold. Combine that with California's high property values pushing many investors past conventional loan limits, and DSCR has become less of a niche product and more of a default strategy for scaling a portfolio here.

Short-term rental investors in particular have benefited, since several DSCR programs now allow projected STR income (via AirDNA or similar) rather than requiring a full year of lease history.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Underestimating PITIA: Forgetting HOA dues or flood insurance can push your DSCR below the threshold at the last minute.
  • Assuming all lenders calculate rent the same way: Some use actual signed leases, others use market rent from an appraisal (Form 1007). Know which applies to your file before you make an offer.
  • Not shopping the entity vesting rules: Not every DSCR lender treats multi-member LLCs the same way — worth confirming early if you're investing with partners.

Is a DSCR Loan Right for You?

If your rental income covers the mortgage and you'd rather not hand over two years of tax returns, a DSCR loan is likely your fastest path to closing. It's not the right fit for every buyer — if you qualify easily under conventional guidelines and want the lowest possible rate, conventional financing may still win. But for investors scaling a portfolio or working around self-employment income, DSCR has become the standard tool for a reason.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a minimum credit score for a DSCR loan?
Most programs require a 660 minimum, though scores of 700+ typically unlock better pricing and lower down payment requirements.
Can I use projected short-term rental income to qualify?
Yes — many DSCR programs now accept projected STR income from tools like AirDNA instead of requiring a signed long-term lease.
Can I close a DSCR loan in my LLC's name?
In most cases, yes. This is one of the key advantages over conventional financing, which typically requires the loan to close in your personal name.
